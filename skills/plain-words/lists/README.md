# Word lists

`ngsl-1.2.csv` and `ngsl-supplement.csv` are the New General Service List 1.2
("lemmatized for research", every inflected form of each lemma) and its
supplementary words, by Browne, C., Culligan, B., and Phillips, J., licensed
under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Source:
<https://www.newgeneralservicelist.com/new-general-service-list>. The only
changes are Unix line endings and, in the supplement, a removed byte-order mark.
These two files stay under CC BY-SA 4.0, not the repository's MIT license.

`blocked.txt` lists words dropped from the downloaded xkcd list.

The American Oxford 3000 and the xkcd Simple Writer list are not committed.
`plain-words fetch` downloads them.
