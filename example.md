# Proposal: move the team to a four-day week

We propose that the team work four ten-hour days instead of five eight-hour days, starting next quarter.

## Why

Every company that has tried a four-day week reports higher output. A long weekend lets people recover, so they come back more focused and make fewer mistakes. Our own survey found that 90% of the team want the change, which shows it will raise productivity.

Fewer workdays also cut costs. With the office closed on Fridays, we save on heating, cleaning, and catering, and these savings will be large.

## Plan

Each person picks Monday or Friday as their day off, so the the team is covered all five days. Managers will track output for one quarter and compare it against the previous quarter. If output does not fall, we keep the schedule.

## Risks

Customers might recieve slower replies on the day a person is out. Ten-hour days could also be tiring, but this is unlikely to matter.
